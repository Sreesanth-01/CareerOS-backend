# 🚀 CareerOS

> All-in-one platform to organize and accelerate your career journey.

## 📖 About

CareerOS is a full-stack application designed to help students, freshers, and professionals manage every aspect of their career in one place. From tracking job applications and managing projects to organizing resumes and setting career goals, CareerOS aims to become a personal operating system for career growth.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

* 🎓 Students
* 👨‍💻 Freshers
* 💼 Job Seekers
* 🚀 Professionals looking to track and improve their career journey
